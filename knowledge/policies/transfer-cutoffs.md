## Business days
Our business days are Monday through Friday, except Federal Reserve holidays. Transfers requested after a cutoff time, or on a day that is not a business day, are processed on the next business day.

## Domestic wire cutoff
Outgoing domestic wires approved by 4:00 pm Eastern Time on a business day are sent the same day. Wires approved later are sent the next business day. Incoming domestic wires are credited the day we receive them.

## International wire cutoff
Outgoing international wires approved by 2:00 pm Eastern Time on a business day are sent the same day. Delivery to the recipient usually takes one to five business days, depending on the destination country and intermediary banks.

## ACH transfer cutoffs
Standard ACH transfers approved by 7:00 pm Eastern Time arrive at the receiving bank on the next business day. Same-day ACH transfers approved by 1:45 pm Eastern Time arrive the same business day, for transfers up to $100,000. Transfers proposed by an AI agent are not sent until you approve them in the Agentic Bank app, and the cutoff applies to the time of your approval.

## Cancelling a transfer
You can cancel a pending transfer in the Agentic Bank app until it is sent. Once a wire is sent it usually cannot be cancelled, but you can ask us to request its return. A sent ACH transfer can be reversed only in limited cases, such as a duplicate or wrong amount.
