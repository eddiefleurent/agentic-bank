## Lost or stolen card
If your debit card is lost or stolen, lock it right away in the Agentic Bank app, then report it in the app or by phone. Reporting it cancels the card number and orders a replacement with a new number. Locking alone keeps the same number, so you can unlock the card if you find it.

## Replacement timing and cost
A standard replacement card is free and arrives in 7 to 10 business days. Expedited delivery costs $25.00 and arrives in 2 business days. Premier Checking customers get expedited delivery free.

## Damaged or expiring cards
If your card is damaged or worn, request a replacement in the app. A damaged card replacement keeps the same card number and is free. We mail a new card about 30 days before your current card expires.

## Recurring payments and digital wallets
When a card is replaced because it was lost or stolen, recurring payments linked to the old number may fail. Update the card number with each merchant. Cards in digital wallets update automatically within one business day.
