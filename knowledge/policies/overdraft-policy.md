## What an overdraft is
An overdraft happens when you do not have enough available money in your account to cover a transaction, but we pay it anyway. Whether we pay an overdraft is at our discretion. We do not pay overdrafts on accounts that are past due or under review.

## Overdraft fee
We charge an overdraft fee of $29.00 for each item we pay into overdraft. We charge at most three overdraft fees per business day, for a maximum of $87.00 per day. We do not charge a fee if your account is overdrawn by $50.00 or less at the end of the business day, or for any single item of $5.00 or less.

## Grace period
If your account is overdrawn, you have until 11:59 pm Eastern Time on the next business day to bring the available balance to at least $0.00. If you do, we waive the overdraft fees for the previous business day.

## ATM and everyday debit card transactions
We do not pay ATM withdrawals or one-time debit card purchases into overdraft unless you opt in. You can opt in or out at any time in the Agentic Bank app. If you have not opted in, those transactions are declined at no charge. Checks, ACH payments and recurring debit card payments can still be paid into overdraft whether or not you opt in.

## Overdraft protection transfers
You can link an Agentic Bank savings account as overdraft protection. When your checking account would be overdrawn, we transfer the amount needed from savings. Each overdraft protection transfer costs $10.00, which is less than the overdraft fee.

## Returned items
If we decline to pay an item, we return it unpaid. We do not charge a fee for returned (non-sufficient funds) items.
