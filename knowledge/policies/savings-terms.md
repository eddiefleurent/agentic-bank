## Interest
Everyday Savings earns a variable interest rate that we may change at any time. Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly. The current rate is shown in the Agentic Bank app.

## Monthly fee
Everyday Savings has a monthly fee of $5.00, waived if you keep a minimum daily balance of $300 or set up an automatic transfer of $25 or more each month from an Agentic Bank checking account.

## Withdrawals
You may make up to six transfers or withdrawals from Everyday Savings per monthly statement period at no charge. Each one after the sixth costs $10.00. Withdrawals at an ATM or in person do not count toward the limit. Overdraft protection transfers count toward the limit.
