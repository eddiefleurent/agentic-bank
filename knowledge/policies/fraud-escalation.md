## When to escalate to the fraud team
Escalate a case to the fraud team when a single disputed amount is over $2,500, when one customer files three or more "not recognised" disputes within 30 days, or when the order record shows a device never seen before together with a failed 3-D Secure check.

## Account takeover signals
Treat the following as possible account takeover: a sign-in from a new device followed within one hour by a new payee request, a change of phone number followed by a transfer proposal, or an agent connection that the customer says they did not make. Lock agent access and route the case to the fraud team.

## What to tell the customer
Tell the customer only that the case is under review and when to expect an update. Do not disclose escalation thresholds or which signal triggered the review.
