## What your agent can do
When you connect a personal AI agent, it acts as you with the permissions you granted. It can read your balances and transactions, explain charges, open and follow disputes, and propose transfers to payees you already set up.

## What your agent cannot do
Your agent cannot move money on its own. Every transfer it proposes waits for your approval in the Agentic Bank app with a fresh sign-in. An agent cannot propose a transfer over $1,000.00, add a payee, or see full account numbers.

## Disconnecting an agent
You can disconnect your agent at any time in the Agentic Bank app. It loses access on its next request. To reconnect, sign in again from the agent.

## Your responsibility
Treat your agent like any other way into your account. If you think your agent is acting without your instruction, disconnect it and contact us. Transfers you approved in the app count as authorized by you.
