## Monthly maintenance fee
Everyday Checking has a monthly maintenance fee of $8.00. The fee is waived for any statement period in which you keep a minimum daily balance of $1,500, receive direct deposits totaling $500 or more, or are under 25 years old. Premier Checking has a monthly fee of $25.00, waived with combined balances of $15,000 or more.

## ATM fees
Agentic Bank ATMs are free. Using another bank's ATM in the United States costs $2.50 per withdrawal or balance inquiry, plus any fee the ATM owner charges. Premier Checking refunds up to four non-Agentic Bank ATM fees per statement period.

## Wire transfer fees
Incoming domestic wires cost $15.00. Outgoing domestic wires cost $25.00. Outgoing international wires cost $45.00. Incoming international wires cost $16.00. Premier Checking customers pay no fee for incoming wires.

## Other service fees
Stop payment order: $30.00. Cashier's check: $10.00. Paper statements: $2.00 per month (electronic statements are free). Returned deposited item: $12.00. Account research beyond 12 months: $25.00 per hour. Expedited debit card delivery is listed in the card replacement policy.
