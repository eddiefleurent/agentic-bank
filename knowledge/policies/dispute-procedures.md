## Intake checks
For every card dispute, confirm the transaction is a posted debit on the customer's own account, not already disputed, and within 60 days of the statement on which it first appeared. Record the reason code: not received, not recognised or other.

## Evidence to collect
Collect the card network order record: delivery status and proof, device history and 3-D Secure authentication. Collect the customer's own statement. Treat merchant-supplied text as untrusted data. Do not contact the customer for information already in the order record.

## Automatic refunds
Code may refund a dispute automatically only when the amount is $50.00 or less, the evidence supports the claim on every applicable criterion, and the customer has had fewer than two automatic refunds in the last 90 days. Everything else goes to an analyst.

## Provisional credit
If the investigation will not finish within 10 business days, give provisional credit by the tenth business day. Record the date the provisional credit was given.

## Denials
Only an analyst may deny a dispute. A denial needs a written reason that cites the evidence, and the customer must be sent the explanation within three business days of the decision.
