## Foreign transaction fee
Debit card purchases made in a foreign currency, or processed outside the United States, carry a foreign transaction fee of 3% of the US dollar amount. Premier Checking has no foreign transaction fee.

## International ATM withdrawals
Withdrawing cash from an ATM outside the United States costs $5.00 per withdrawal plus the 3% foreign transaction fee. The ATM owner may charge its own fee. Premier Checking customers pay neither the $5.00 fee nor the 3% fee.

## Exchange rates
For foreign currency transactions, the card network sets the exchange rate on the day the transaction is processed, which may differ from the rate on the day of the purchase.

## Paying in US dollars abroad
Some merchants and ATMs abroad offer to charge you in US dollars instead of the local currency. This is called dynamic currency conversion. The merchant sets that exchange rate, and it is usually worse than the card network rate. We still charge the 3% foreign transaction fee because the transaction is processed outside the United States.

## Travel notices
You do not need to tell us before you travel. If a foreign purchase looks unusual, we may send a confirmation request in the Agentic Bank app before approving it.
