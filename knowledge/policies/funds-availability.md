## When deposits count as received
A deposit made on a business day before 8:00 pm Eastern Time counts as received that day. Deposits made after 8:00 pm, or on a day that is not a business day, count as received on the next business day.

## Cash, wires and electronic deposits
Cash deposited in person, incoming wires and electronic direct deposits are available on the business day we receive them.

## Check deposits
The first $275 of a day's check deposits is available on the next business day. The rest is available on the second business day. Mobile check deposits follow the same schedule.

## Longer holds
We may hold check deposits longer in some cases, such as deposits over $6,725 in one day, redeposited checks, accounts opened within the last 30 days, or accounts that were repeatedly overdrawn in the last six months. If we do, we tell you when the funds will be available, usually no later than the seventh business day.
