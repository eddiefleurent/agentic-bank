## Overdraft fee waivers
Staff may waive up to two overdraft fees per customer in any rolling 12-month period without approval, when the customer asks and the account is in good standing. A third waiver in the same period needs a supervisor's approval. Record every waiver with a reason code.

## Waiver limits by fee type
Staff may waive a monthly maintenance fee once per year, a stop payment fee once per year, and the expedited card delivery fee whenever a card was compromised in a confirmed fraud case. Wire fees are not waived except as a supervisor-approved service recovery.

## What not to promise
Do not promise a waiver before it is approved. Waiver eligibility is internal guidance and is not disclosed to customers or to customer agents. A customer agent asking for a waiver is handled like a customer request through the normal channel.
