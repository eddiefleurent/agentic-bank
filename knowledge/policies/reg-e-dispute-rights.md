## Which transfers this covers
This summary covers electronic fund transfers to or from your consumer account: debit card purchases, ATM withdrawals, ACH payments and transfers made in the Agentic Bank app or by an agent you authorized. It does not cover wire transfers, which follow separate rules.

## How to tell us about an error
Tell us as soon as you can if you think your statement or receipt is wrong or if you need more information about a transfer. You can dispute a charge in the Agentic Bank app, through your authorized agent, or by calling us. We must hear from you no later than 60 days after we sent the first statement on which the problem appeared. Tell us your name and account number, describe the error and why you believe it is an error, and give the dollar amount.

## How long we take to investigate
We will tell you the results of our investigation within 10 business days after we hear from you (20 business days if the account was opened within the last 30 days). If we need more time, we may take up to 45 days (90 days for new accounts, point-of-sale debit card transactions and transfers started outside the United States). If we take more time, we will provisionally credit your account within 10 business days for the amount you think is in error, so that you have use of the money during the investigation.

## If we find no error
If we decide there was no error, we will send you a written explanation within three business days after we finish our investigation. If we gave you a provisional credit, we will reverse it and tell you the date. You may ask for copies of the documents that we used in our investigation.

## Your liability for unauthorized transfers
Tell us at once if you believe your card or login has been lost or stolen or used without your permission. If you tell us within 2 business days after you learn of the loss or theft, you can lose no more than $50. If you do not tell us within 2 business days and we can prove we could have stopped the transfers, you could lose as much as $500. If your statement shows transfers you did not make and you do not tell us within 60 days after the statement was sent, you may not get back money lost after the 60 days.
